# Notifications, inbox and retention

## Push and in-app notifications

- **Earn the permission.** Pre-prompt in your own UI at the moment the value is obvious; only then trigger the system dialog.
- **Every notification is about something the user chose** to follow or asked for. Tie them to the user's own items (their order, their project, their saved search).
- **Group related notifications** (one per thread or item) instead of stacking ten separate alerts.
- **Tapping opens the exact thing**, not the home screen.
- **Settings per category** with sensible defaults; one tap to mute a category from the notification itself.
- **Quiet hours and frequency caps.** More than a few per day per category is a reason to uninstall.
- **Never send test or admin messages to real users.** Keep test sends on a separate audience and environment.

## Inbox / activity

- Newest first, read and unread states, mark all as read.
- Each item says what happened, to what, and when, and links to it.
- Empty inbox explains what will show up here.

## Glanceable surfaces (widgets, lock screen, wearables)

- Show the one thing the user follows, at a glance, with the brand mark small.
- Update only on a real change, with a periodic heartbeat; never drain battery with constant refreshes.
- Tapping opens the matching screen in the app.

## Email

- Transactional email (receipts, codes, resets) is instant, plain and on-brand.
- Lifecycle email (welcome, win-back, digests) has one goal and one link, respects unsubscribe immediately, and never repeats.

## Retention basics

- A reason to come back daily that is real (fresh content, a result, a streak that means something), not guilt.
- Saved state everywhere: drafts, filters, scroll position, half-finished flows.
- Share and invite flows that produce a link worth opening: a preview card with real content, not a generic logo.
